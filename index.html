Methodology & scope notes

Data source

Every unsolved ticket (New, Open, Pending or On Hold) in the Zendesk ePayment Team group (group_id 25484726460951), plus unsolved tickets in any other group whose CORE-Module is ePayments or whose Ticket Status Detail is "Escalated::External Teams::Payrix". Tickets come from the support team's shared Zendesk store, which reads Zendesk's change feed every 2 minutes. This page doesn't pull anything itself: it reads that store, the shared Jira store, and the shared Payrix merchant store. Flags and hours are worked out in your browser, so they stay current between refreshes.

Escalated to Payrix

  • A ticket counts when its Ticket Status Detail field is Escalated::External Teams::Payrix (tag escalated___payrix). There is no API into Payrix's own support desk (wp4p.zendesk.com) yet, so this board can't see Payrix's side of the request.
  • These tickets wait on Payrix, so like GAL tickets they sit in their own section and are left out of the flagged views. A review hides one from the open list for 7 days.
  • The 180-day list includes solved tickets. A ticket only shows there if the field still says Payrix; when an agent changes the detail on solving (for example to Solved::Troubleshooting) the ticket drops out, so the history undercounts. Zendesk doesn't keep when the field was set, so "days open" is from ticket creation.

From Payrix / Worldpay

  • Tickets whose requester email is at wp4p.zendesk.com (Payrix Boarding and Risk), payrix.helpscoutapp.com, payrix.com, worldpay.com, globalpay.com or globalpayments.com. These are requests from the processor to BQE.
  • They stay in the flagged views: a reply to these goes to Payrix, and it still counts as our turn.

Flag definitions

  • No agent reply yet: open 4+ hours with zero public comment from anyone other than the requester.
  • Awaiting our reply: the requester's comment is the most recent public comment, 4+ hours ago.
  • Heavy back-and-forth: 10+ public comments. Shown for a look, not urgent by itself.
  • Recently checked in: the newest comment is an internal note from the last 24 hours. The ticket leaves the flagged views until something newer happens.

Urgency tiers

  • Needs attention now: flagged, last activity within 48 hours.
  • Aging: flagged, last activity 2–7 days ago.
  • Long-stale backlog: flagged, last activity 7+ days ago.
  • Hours are calendar time, not business hours.

Payrix merchant check

  • Merchant data comes from the Payrix API (read-only, refreshed every 6 hours): status, frozen, inactive, last activity and total approved sales.
  • A ticket is matched to a merchant when the Zendesk organization name equals the merchant's DBA or legal name after dropping punctuation and suffixes like Inc, LLC and PC, or when the requester's email is the merchant's email. No match doesn't mean the customer has no merchant account, only that the names differ.
  • Frozen, closed or inactive merchants, and merchants with no activity for 30+ days, are highlighted. Payrix status codes: 2 boarded, 3 manual, 4 closed.

With Dev Team (GAL) and ES

  • A GAL-##### in the subject puts a ticket in With Dev Team; it's left out of every other section. GAL chips show the issue's Support Ranking, and "Missing SR" when it has none.
  • ES Escalations (ePayments) are open Jira ES issues with the component ePayments, BQE, linked to Zendesk through the ES Number field. Reviews there last for the day.

Reviewed checkbox & notes

  • Shared by everyone who can open this board, with who changed them last. They stay on this board; nothing is written to Zendesk, Jira or Payrix.
  • GAL, escalated-to-Payrix and heavy back-and-forth reviews last 7 days; ES reviews last the day; other reviews last until someone rechecks them.

Refresh

  • The page refreshes itself every 15 minutes while it's open and on screen, and right away when you come back to it after that. Ticket changes show up within a few minutes; merchant data within 6 hours.
  • The trend chart gets one point per hour on weekdays, starting 10/22/2026.
